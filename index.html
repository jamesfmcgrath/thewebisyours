A grassroots web campaign

Every business deserves a home on the web they own.

We're tired of searching for a local business and finding only a Facebook page last updated in 2022. So we're doing something about it. Free one-page websites for small businesses that only exist on social media. Starting in the west of Ireland. One business at a time.
