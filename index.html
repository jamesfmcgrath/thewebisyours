A grassroots web campaign

Every business deserves a home on the web they own.

We're tired of searching for a local business and finding only a Facebook page last updated in 2022. So we're doing something about it. Free one-page websites for small businesses that only exist on social media. Starting in the west of Ireland, with a simple goal — one site a month. One business at a time.

How can we help?

🏪

Know a Business?

A café, a trades person, a music teacher, a shop. If you know a local business whose only web presence is a Facebook page, tell us. We want to help them.

🌱

Own a Business?

We build you a free one-page website. We buy your domain. We host it free for the first year. After that it's yours to keep for the price of a few coffees a year.

💻

Are You a Developer?

One business. One page. One month of your time. We have a template, a process, and a community forming. Come build something that matters.

The web was built for everyone. Somewhere along the way, small businesses were told a Facebook page was enough. It isn't. Your story deserves a home you actually own.
