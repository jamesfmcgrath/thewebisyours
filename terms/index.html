Who We Are

The Web Is Yours is a grassroots campaign run by James McGrath, trading as JFM Digital Works, Westport, Co. Mayo, Ireland.

Contact: hello@thewebisyours.org

What We Offer

We build a free one-page website for small businesses that currently have no website of their own. As part of this offer we:

  • Design and build a single one-page website using our standard template
  • Purchase and register a domain name in the business's name (~€15 value)
  • Host the website free of charge on Cloudflare Pages for the first 12 months
  • Make minor text corrections within 30 days of launch

This is a voluntary campaign. We offer this service free of charge and in good faith.

What We Do Not Offer

The following are explicitly outside the scope of this campaign:

  • More than one page
  • Ecommerce or online shop functionality
  • Booking or reservation systems
  • Ongoing content updates beyond the 30-day correction period
  • Search engine optimisation (SEO) services
  • Social media management
  • Email setup or hosting
  • Ongoing technical support or maintenance

If your business requires any of the above we are happy to point you towards a suitable local developer or agency.
